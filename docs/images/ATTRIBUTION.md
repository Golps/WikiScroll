## Swipe demos

The swipe GIFs in `docs/images/` and MP4 files in `docs/demos/` were recorded on October 29, 2026 with the same cards: Chocolate Hills to Arp 273 on desktop (right arrow), and Long-eared owl to Arp 273 on mobile (touch swipe). They use real keyboard and touch events with the reader. Capture is frame by frame: the app's clock and animations advance in exact 1/60-second steps, so MP4 files are 60 fps and inline GIFs 50 fps. They are deterministic UI demonstrations, not a measurement of device frame rates.

Images are resized and positioned by the actual reader layout. The documentation photos are not AI-generated. Screenshots and GIFs contain no EXIF, workstation paths or embedded capture logs. The WikiScroll name and logo retain their separate branding terms in the main README.
