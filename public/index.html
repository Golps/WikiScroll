Replace doomscrolling with discovery

A spare moment can leave you with something worth knowing. WikiScroll turns Wikipedia knowledge and Wikivoyage travel guides into a thoughtful, swipeable reader.

Instead of another stream of reactions and headlines, discover an unfamiliar idea, a surprising life, or a place you have never considered visiting. Read a short introduction, follow the original article when something catches your attention, and stop whenever you like.

Follow your curiosity, not an engagement score

Your likes save what matters to you. They do not train a feed around your last swipe. Explore broadly or choose topics deliberately. There are no daily targets or streaks to protect, and no account is required.

Two ways to explore

  • Wikipedia: discover people, science, history, nature, technology, culture, and more. Select topics to focus your discovery. Adjust article depth to favor familiar subjects or more obscure finds.
  • Wikivoyage: discover destinations through travel guides. Filter by country or region and trip style, then open the map to put a place in context. Travel controls are separate from Wikipedia topics and depth.

Keep what stays with you

  • Saved articles: swipe right or press Save to keep an interesting discovery. Swipe left to pass.
  • Collections: organize discoveries into reading lists and share a collection through a link, with a preview designed for messages and social apps.
  • Reading history: find articles you have already encountered.
  • Gentle activity stats: see today's article count and a weekly activity chart, tracked separately for Wikipedia and Wikivoyage, without streaks or daily goals.

A reader that fits your day

  • 15 language choices: English is the default. Also choose Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Korean, Hebrew, Dutch, or Polish. Article availability depends on the source language.
  • Dark and light themes: choose the appearance that suits you.
  • Ambient reading: press Spacebar on desktop or long-press a card on mobile to focus on a discovery with fewer distractions. Press Spacebar again, or tap the background or text, to return.
  • Phone, tablet, and desktop: responsive cards, touch gestures, desktop keyboard controls, and two-finger horizontal trackpad swipes.
  • Home-screen installation: install WikiScroll as a Progressive Web App for quick access.
  • Cached reading: previously cached content can remain available offline. New discoveries and maps need a connection.
  • Ready for the next discovery: cards load ahead as you read to keep moving between articles smooth.

Your discoveries stay yours

Preferences, saved articles, collections, and reading history are stored in your browser. There is no account-based sync. If you share a collection, anyone with its link can read that snapshot. Clearing browser data may remove your local library.

Built on open knowledge

WikiScroll is an independent reader, not an official Wikimedia Foundation product. Article content comes from Wikipedia and Wikivoyage. Every card links to its original article for the full story, attribution, and licensing information.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What WikiScroll is. WikiScroll is a free, client-side web application that displays random articles from Wikipedia and Wikivoyage. It is hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

Data we collect. WikiScroll does not require an account or automatically upload your saved collections. If you share a collection, its selected contents are included in the link and processed by Cloudflare to display the collection and its preview image. We do not set tracking cookies or use advertising SDKs. When enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics provides cookieless, non-identifying measurements of visits and performance; it does not receive your saved articles, collections, or reading history. We do not know who you are.

Local storage. Your preferences (liked articles, reading history, settings, theme, and collections) are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB. This data stays on your device unless you choose to share a collection. Shared links contain the selected collection name, article titles, excerpts, and article identifiers. Anyone with the link, and the messaging service generating its preview, can read that snapshot. Links cannot be revoked; deleting a local collection does not delete a previously shared snapshot. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Third-party services. WikiScroll loads content from the following external services:

  • Wikipedia / Wikivoyage / Wikimedia APIs: Article content, images, and pageview statistics. Subject to the Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Hosting and CDN delivery. Cloudflare may collect standard web server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps) as part of their infrastructure. Subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Leaflet, OpenStreetMap / Nominatim, CARTO and cdnjs: map software, map tiles and delivery. Maps use Wikivoyage's own coordinates; when a guide has none, its place name is sent to Nominatim to locate it. Requests are subject to each provider’s privacy policy.

Service Worker. WikiScroll registers a service worker to cache articles for offline use. This caching is entirely local to your device.

Changes to this policy. WikiScroll reserves the right to update, modify, or replace this privacy policy at any time and for any reason. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of WikiScroll after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.

Contact. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@example.com.
