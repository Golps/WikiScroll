# Screenshot attribution

The screenshots in this folder were taken from the live site, [wikiscroll.com](https://wikiscroll.com), in September 2026. They were captured in a fresh browser profile. The small reading counts in the settings screenshot, the saved article in the swipe animation and the "Remarkable objects" collection are demo data, created for these screenshots.

- **Map tiles** show © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors and © [CARTO](https://carto.com/attributions).
