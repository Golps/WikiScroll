## Swipe demos

The swipe GIFs in `docs/images/` and MP4 files in `docs/demos/` were captured on October 21, 2026, before the latest interface polish. They use actual keyboard and touch events with the reader; frame-by-frame capture advances the app clock at 60 samples per second, MP4 files preserve 60 fps and inline GIFs use 50 fps. They are deterministic UI demonstrations, not a measurement of device frame rates. Their articles are [Glaucus atlanticus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glaucus_atlanticus) and [STEVE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STEVE), with photographs by [Sylke Rohrlach, CC BY-SA 2.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blue_dragon-glaucus_atlanticus_(8599051974).jpg) and [Krista Trinder, via NASA Goddard, CC0 as listed on Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Aurora_Named_STEVE_(26938621338).jpg).

Images are resized and positioned by the actual reader layout. The documentation photos are not AI-generated. Screenshots and GIFs contain no EXIF, workstation paths or embedded capture logs. The WikiScroll name and logo retain their separate branding terms in the main README.
